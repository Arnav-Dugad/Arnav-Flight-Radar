Arnav Flight Radar

The Apex Vector. The letter A is drawn as an aviation heading chevron, and its apex is also the radar origin. The crossbar isn’t a line: it is a range ring centred on that apex, rendered as a time-faded flight trail. The trail ends at one amber aircraft, the tracked target. The mark says letter, radar and live flight at once, with no stock icons.

  1. Chevron: heading bug and the letter A
  2. Apex: radar origin; all rings share this centre
  3. Crossbar: range ring as a flight trail, older positions fade
  4. Aircraft: original silhouette, heading tangent to the ring
  5. Beam: a soft cyan scan lighting the inside of the A
